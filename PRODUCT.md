# PRODUCT.md — Anand Bakery (Ananth Bakery)

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Anand Bakery (Official heritage name: **Ananth Bakery**)
- **Established:** 1968 (58 Years of Heritage)
- **Tagline:** *A Taste of Heritage — Since 1968*
- **Location:** Main Road, Sawyerpuram, Thoothukudi (Tuticorin), Tamil Nadu
- **Phone:** +91 95668 08729 / +91 98942 97396
- **Instagram:** @ananth_bakery

## The Signature Hero
- **The Star Cake:** An icon of Sawyerpuram since 1968. Baked with **100% pure cold-pressed coconut oil**, golden star flower silhouette, airy spongy texture that melts with nostalgic warmth.
- **The Hot Americano:** Frothy, aromatic South Indian filter-espresso brew served in vintage glass tumblers, celebrated across town as the best coffee in Sawyerpuram.

## Audience & Purpose
- **Mode:** Persuade & Experience (Editorial Heritage Showcase)
- **Target Audience:** Locals, nostalgic travellers, foodies, culinary connoisseurs, and visitors travelling across Tamil Nadu looking for authentic, generational wood-fired bakes.
- **Primary Goal:** Transform the bakery's authentic local legacy into a world-class, avant-garde digital culinary experience that honors three generations of craftsmanship.
